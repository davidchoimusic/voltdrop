Start with the insulation column, correct for heat and bundled conductors, then see exactly which safety limit controls.

AWG = American Wire Gauge. It is printed on the wire jacket.

Use the conductor insulation rating for the starting column. THHN is normally 90°C. This is not the termination temperature rating.

Use 60°C for NM-B or unmarked equipment. Use 75°C or 90°C only when the equipment is marked and listed for that temperature. The termination limit is applied after both derating factors.

Enter the surrounding air temperature at the conductors. NEC Table 310.15(B)(1) publishes bands through 85°C; a “—” cell is not permitted and this calculator will not substitute a value.

conductors

Count ungrounded conductors and any neutral carrying current under normal conditions, including neutrals carrying harmonics from non-linear loads. Do not count equipment grounding or grounding electrode conductors. Count spare conductors. Do not count conductors that cannot be energized at the same time.
