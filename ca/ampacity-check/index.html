What this checks (and what it doesn't)

We look up your wire in the Canadian ampacity tables (CEC Table 2 for copper and Table 4 for aluminum; the sealed values are harmonized with NEC Table 310.16). For small wires we also apply CEC Rule 14-104: 14 AWG copper tops out at a 15 A breaker, 12 AWG at 20 A, and 10 AWG at 30 A.

Not included: hot attics/engine rooms, bundles of more than three current-carrying wires, and continuous loads (running 3+ hours straight needs 25% headroom). Those all reduce what the wire can carry — when in doubt, go up a size or ask an electrician.

Ampacity and voltage drop are separate checks — a wire must pass both.
