🔌 What Size Wire for a 30 Amp Breaker in Canada?

🇺🇸 US version

The short answer

Start with 10 AWG copper NMD90 for an ordinary 30 A circuit. A TT-30 RV receptacle is 120 V, while a typical dryer or water-heater circuit is 240 V; that voltage difference changes long-run sizing.

The verified 60°C column supports #8 aluminum at 35 A, so aluminum is included. Confirm the conductor type, terminals, and equipment listing in the Canadian Ampacity Check tool. Ampacity Check tool.

30-amp wire size FAQ

What size wire for a 30 amp RV plug in Canada?
A TT-30 RV receptacle is 120 V. Start with 10 AWG copper NMD90 at ordinary distances, then use the 120 V table or calculator for the actual one-way run.
Can 12 gauge wire handle 30 amps in Canada?
No. CEC Rule 14-104 caps 12 AWG copper at a 20 A overcurrent device for ordinary circuits, so it does not pass a 30 A ampacity check.
What size wire does a 30 amp dryer use in Canada?
Start with 10 AWG copper NMD90 for an ordinary 30 A dryer circuit. Check the appliance instructions, termination rule, and distance before installation.
